# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

MindBridge welcomes respectful, constructive, and professional collaboration.

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Giving actionable, respectful feedback.
- Assuming good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Keeping discussions focused on the work.
- Being considerate when discussing sensitive mental health topics.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory or exclusionary language.
- Trolling, bad-faith arguments, or repeated disruption.
- Sharing private or sensitive personal data without consent.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately using the reporting instructions in `SECURITY.md`. Please do not post sensitive personal information in public issues.

Maintainers will review reports and take appropriate action based on the circumstances.
